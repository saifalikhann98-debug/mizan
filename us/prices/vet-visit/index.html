How much does a routine vet exam cost in the US?

A routine vet exam is typically advertised between $70 and $175 per visit, with most quotes around $100 — figures as of September 2026, from published 2025–26 US cost guides (Angi, HomeGuide, HomeAdvisor, Thumbtack, StyleSeat, CareCredit, GoodRx and others). Your metro, the provider and what's included all move the price, so the advertised rate is rarely the price you have to pay.
