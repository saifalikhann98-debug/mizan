How much does hourly house cleaning cost in the US?

Hourly house cleaning is typically advertised between $25 and $75 per hour, with most quotes around $45 — figures as of September 2026, from published 2025–26 US cost guides (Angi, HomeGuide, HomeAdvisor, Thumbtack, StyleSeat, CareCredit, GoodRx and others). Your metro, the provider and what's included all move the price, so the advertised rate is rarely the price you have to pay.
