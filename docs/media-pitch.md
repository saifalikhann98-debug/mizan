## Pitch A — the RERA rent-increase calculator (send now)

**Subject:** Free calculator: is your Dubai landlord's rent increase actually legal?

> Hi <Name>,
>
> Renewal season is coming and the same question floods every Dubai group every year: *"my landlord
> wants +15% — is that legal?"* Most tenants don't know the answer is fixed by Decree 43/2013 and
> depends only on how far their rent sits below the RERA market average (≤10% below → 0% increase;
> 11–20% → max 5%; up to 20% at most), with 90 days' notice.
>
> I built a free, no-sign-up calculator that does the slab maths and prefills the market average for
> 31 communities: **https://www.mizan-price.com/rent?tab=legal** (Arabic: /ar/rent). It links to the
> official DLD index for the authoritative figure — it's a sanity check, not legal advice.
>
> Happy to give you a quick explainer of the slabs, a couple of worked examples (Marina 1-bed, JVC
> 2-bed), or an embeddable version. No ads, no agents, no listings on the site — it's a resident-built
> tool.
>
> Best,
> Saif
> mizan-price.com

**If they bite — have ready:** three worked examples (current rent → market avg → % below → max legal
increase → max new rent), the slab table (it's on `/rent`), and one line on who you are.

## Boilerplate (paste when asked "what is Mizan?")
> Mizan (ميزان, "scales") is a free, resident-built fair-price checker for the UAE. It shows the
> advertised price range for a service, a rental or a car repair next to what residents report actually
> paying — the gap between the two is the point. Prices are added anonymously; the "paid" layer only
> appears once five or more people have reported, with outliers trimmed and per-device limits so no
> business can pump its own range. Bilingual (English/Arabic), no ads, no paid placements.
