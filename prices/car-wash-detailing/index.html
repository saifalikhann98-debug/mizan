How much does car wash & detailing cost in Dubai?

Car wash & detailing in Dubai is typically advertised between AED 30 and AED 600 per service, with most quotes around AED 150. Actual prices swing with your area, the provider, and what is included, so the advertised rate is rarely the price you have to pay.
